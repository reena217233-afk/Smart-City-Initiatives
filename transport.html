Transport Facilities

Transportation is a cornerstone of our Smart City initiative, designed to make mobility faster, safer, and more eco‑friendly. We are introducing smart traffic management systems powered by AI to reduce congestion and improve road safety. Modern public transit options such as metro networks, electric buses, and integrated ticketing platforms ensure seamless travel across the city. Eco‑mobility solutions like bicycle‑sharing programs, pedestrian‑friendly zones, and electric vehicle infrastructure promote sustainable commuting. With GPS‑enabled tracking and real‑time updates, our transport facilities aim to provide citizens with reliable, efficient, and environmentally responsible mobility choices.
